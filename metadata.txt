about=The GeoLogic Flight Planner is a professional-grade QGIS plugin designed for efficient drone mapping and aerial photogrammetry. It integrates spatial analysis with drone kinematics to generate high-efficiency flight paths that automatically adapt to terrain via Digital Elevation Models (DEMs). The plugin bridges the gap between GIS and field operations by translating complex mapping logic into native DJI WPML-compliant KMZ missions. Key features include intelligent waypoint densification to safeguard controller memory, kinematic-based flight time estimation for enhanced safety, and advanced route optimization that minimizes battery consumption and flight time. Built to provide an autonomous, transparent, and robust alternative to proprietary software, it empowers field professionals to plan and execute precise missions directly from their GIS workspace.
